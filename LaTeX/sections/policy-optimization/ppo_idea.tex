\section{Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)}
\begin{frame}{}
    \LARGE Policy Optimization: \textbf{PPO}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{PPO: First-Order Trust Region}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The direct way to keep $\pi_\theta$ close is a hard limit on how far it may move per update, a \textbf{trust region}. TRPO (Schulman et al., 2015) enforces it exactly, but needs second-order optimisation (conjugate gradient, Fisher matrix) --- expensive and complex to implement
    \item \textbf{Question:} can we get the same stability with plain SGD?
    \pause
    \item \textbf{PPO} (Schulman et al., 2017) proposes two variants:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{PPO-KL:} add a KL penalty to the objective and adapt the penalty coefficient $\beta$ over training
        \item \textbf{PPO-Clip:} clip the probability ratio $r_t(\theta)$ to prevent large policy updates
    \end{itemize}
    \item PPO-Clip is the standard variant --- simpler, no hyperparameter tuning on $\beta$, works at least as well empirically
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
