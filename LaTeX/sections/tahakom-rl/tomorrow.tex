\begin{frame}{Tomorrow: Learning the Policy Directly}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.7em
    \item Q-learning learns what every action is worth, then acts greedily. That needs a
          $\max$ over actions, which gets hard when actions are many or continuous, and
          the policy it gives is always deterministic.
    \item \textbf{Policy gradients} skip the values and adjust the policy itself:
          REINFORCE $\rightarrow$ PPO $\rightarrow$ GRPO. You implement GRPO in Lab 3.
    \item Then \textbf{RL in the real world}: RLHF for language models, multi-agent RL and
          robotics.
\end{itemize}
